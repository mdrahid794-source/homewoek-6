Key Ecosystems & Landscapes

•

The Sundarbans:

The Wikipedia entry notes this is the world's largest mangrove forest, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the southwest that protects the coast from cyclones and hosts the Royal Bengal Tiger.
•

Rivers and Waterways:

Often called the "Land of Rivers", the country features a vast network of streams, backwaters, and seasonal wetlands known as haors (such as Tanguar and Hakaluki Haor) that turn into inland seas during the monsoon.
•

Hills & Forests:

The southeastern Chittagong Hill Tracts (Bandarban, Rangamati, Khagrachari) offer green mountain ranges, waterfalls, Kaptai Lake, and rich biodiversity.
•

Tea Gardens & Swamps:

The northeastern region of Sylhet is famous for rolling green tea estates and unique freshwater swamp forests like Ratargul.
•

Coastal Shores:

Home to Cox's Bazar (one of the world's longest natural sandy sea beaches) and Saint Martin's Island with its coral reefs.
